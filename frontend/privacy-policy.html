Privacy Policy

Your privacy and medical information security are our top priorities.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Siva Medicals through our website, contact forms, or prescription upload portal, we may collect:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address.
  • Prescription Documents: Doctor prescription images/PDFs uploaded for order fulfillment.
  • Inquiry Messages: Details of specific medicine requests or health product inquiries.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is strictly utilized to:

  • Process medicine orders and verify medical prescriptions accurately.
  • Coordinate local home delivery or counter pickup at our Madambakkam pharmacy store.
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates via Email and WhatsApp.

3. Data Retention & Prescription Deletion

We respect your data privacy and disk storage integrity. Uploaded prescription files are stored temporarily on secure cloud storage and can be deleted upon request or automatically managed by our pharmacy administrators once the order has been completed.
